# Release notes — hisn 0.6.3

0.6.3 makes the CLI usable from an external service that hosts sessions for
several people. The control protocol stays at revision 3; nothing a 0.6.2
client sends or reads changes.

## For callers

- **Journal cursor.** `hisn journal --after-wseq N` and `--info`; exit 3 when
  the journal was reset, exit 4 on a hole, a warning when retention deleted
  records. No follow mode: callers poll.
- **Machine output.** `--json` on `run --detach`, `stop`, `seat ...` and
  `rootfs list`, one `hisn-error/v1` object for failures, `supervisor_pid` and
  `journal.first_wseq`/`last_wseq` in `ls --json`.
- **Credential on stdin.** `--seat-token-file -` (with `--no-tty` on `exec`).
- **Why it ended.** `session/stop` carries a cause and the status; `exec/exit`
  carries `exec_id`, `pid` and `attach`.
- **Guide.** [docs/EXTERNAL-CALLERS.md](../EXTERNAL-CALLERS.md).

## Security

In open mode the notification engine now fails closed: the catch-all for any
other notified call refuses, and `bind` is decided by the socket's domain
(AF_UNIX and netlink continue, IPv4/IPv6 stream and datagram are executed by
the supervisor, every other domain is refused). AF_ALG and similar domains that
0.6.2 let through are refused in open mode. See ADR 0022.

## Not in this release

Detached exec with status and output by `exec_id` (planned for 0.6.4), cgroup
limits, allowlist mode, inbound access to a port in a session.
